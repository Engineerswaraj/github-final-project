# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Be respectful, welcoming and kind. Do not harass, insult or attack others.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces.

## Enforcement
Report bad behavior to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers may give a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban.
